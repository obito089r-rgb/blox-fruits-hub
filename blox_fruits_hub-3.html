🍎 Fruits

Fruits give your character special abilities. Different fruits suit different play styles.

  • Grinding: Look for moves that hit several enemies and are easy for you to aim.
  • PvP: Consider combos, mobility and how comfortable you are using the moves.
  • Tip: Try a fruit's moves and mastery requirements before spending resources on it.

⚔️ Swords

Swords are a weapon choice for players who want to deal damage with sword moves and basic attacks.

  • Check the sword's NPC, island or puzzle requirement before traveling.
  • Save the required money and materials before attempting to unlock it.
  • Choose a sword that matches your stats and preferred fighting style.

Collection tip: Keep track of swords you own and the ones you still want.

🦈 Races

Each race has its own traits and abilities. Race upgrades can improve what your character can do.

  • Talk to the relevant race-related NPCs to learn available upgrades.
  • Some upgrades require quests, materials or other conditions.
  • Pick a race based on how you play rather than only its appearance.

Remember: Requirements can change after game updates.

🥋 Fighting styles

Fighting styles give you melee moves and are important for building your character.

  • Visit the style teacher and check the price and requirements.
  • Practice the moves and raise mastery to unlock more abilities.
  • Keep your stats and play style in mind when choosing a main style.

🎁 Codes

Codes may give rewards such as experience boosts, stat refunds or in-game titles. They can expire.

  • Open the in-game code/redeem menu.
  • Enter a code exactly as written, then submit it.
  • Use codes only from trustworthy game announcements or verified sources.

Live code list: This demo does not automatically check which codes are active. Confirm a code in-game before sharing it.

⭐ Grinding tips

  • Follow quests close to your current level.
  • Group enemies together when your moves can hit multiple targets.
  • Use the abilities you can control reliably.
  • Spend stat points on the build you actually use.
  • Take breaks and enjoy exploring between quests.
